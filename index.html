Who we are

A student voice rooted in Mukutani

The Ilchamus Student Association (ISA) is the association of Ilchamus students from Mukutani Sub-county in Baringo County, which covers Ilchamus Ward and Mukutani Ward. We exist to serve our community by improving access to education, supporting students in need and building a culture of accountability.

ISA is governed by the ISA Executive, which members elect every year. Our work is education-centred and open to every student, regardless of politics, religion, gender or clan.

At a glance

Our aims and objectives

Taken directly from Article Five of the ISA Constitution: Objects and Powers.

  • Youth empowerment

    Promote youth empowerment in the Ilchamus community.

  • Good governance

    Promote good governance and leadership in our area of jurisdiction.

  • Land and sustainable development

    Promote proper land policies: resource management, equitable distribution and environmental conservation.

  • Educational opportunities

    Awareness and motivation campaigns with county education stakeholders, and advocacy against FGM and early girl-child marriage.

  • HIV/AIDS and drug abuse sensitisation

    Workshops, seminars, dramas, and members volunteering for Voluntary Counselling and Testing (VCT).

  • Cultural preservation

    Discourage retrogressive cultural practices while promoting progressive Ilchamus culture.

Fill in the registration form. Your application is sent to the ISA Executive, who review it and contact you.

Transparency and accountability

ISA is guided by its constitution and by the ISA Executive that members elect.

Constitution

The supreme governing document of the association. Head office: Marigat Town, Baringo County. Area of jurisdiction: Baringo County.

Membership

Open to any student at an institution of higher learning from the Ilchamus community, holding a Kenyan ID or passport. Registration fee: Ksh 150.

Annual General Meeting

Held every 30 December at the headquarters in Marigat, electing office bearers and reviewing accounts.

ISA Executive

The Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, Organizing Secretary, Coordinator, Prayer Coordinator, and 10 Regional Representatives appointed by the Executive.
